# Oriental Fantasy Portrait — Prompt Pattern

Distilled from 2 production prompts that share one 9-block structure. Keep the Chinese phrasing for models that respond well to Chinese (Seedream/Jimeng); the glosses are for adaptation.

## The 9-block template

Every prompt combines nine blocks: (1) subject/style anchor — photorealistic real-person Oriental fantasy portrait; (2) vertical composition; (3) costume palette — 3–4 exact color names plus one accent; (4) fabric physics — liquid silk, pearl-light gauze, highlight behavior, one restraint constraint; (5) precise pose, body-part by body-part; (6) hair and accessories contrasting the costume; (7) face-realism lock (non-negotiable) — real skin texture, natural bone structure and eyes; (8) background and natural light; (9) mood anchor — one feeling, one film reference.

## Adaptation notes

These prompts control look, not identity — pair with a character-consistency skill (sheet as reference) for a recurring heroine. Request vertical aspect (9:16 or 3:4) explicitly. Vary a series by swapping palette, accessories and mood; keep anchor, composition and face lock fixed.
