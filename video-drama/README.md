# AI Video & Short Drama — 16-Skill Production Pack for Muse (Lite Preview)

> **This is a free lite preview.** Each skill folder contains an overview SKILL.md — the core principle and workflow outline — so you can see exactly how the pack works before buying. The complete skills are in the paid full version (see the bottom of this page).

A coordinated set of 16 Muse skills that takes an AI video or short-drama project from a raw story idea to a finished, publishable episode: screenwriting and production planning, directing, character-face consistency, model selection, prompt writing and repair for the major video models, editing, and quality control.

The pack is organized as a pipeline. Each skill has a defined role and hands its output to the next stage, so a one-person studio can run the same workflow a small production team would.

## The 16 skills

### Screenwriting and production planning

- **local-ai-short-drama** — The hub skill: designs, writes, produces and iterates short-drama series season by season, with story architecture, genre engines and a full production pipeline.
- **local-short-drama-agent** — Turns an episode script into production files: extracts character, location, prop and clue assets, then writes per-shot image prompts and video prompts.
- **local-short-drama-prompt-director** — Packages Seedance 2.0 prompts in 4–15 second segments, with beat sheets, scene stills and QA scoring reports.
- **local-short-drama-edit** — Edits generated scenes into a finished episode: in/out points, subtitles and loudness normalization before export.

### Directing and character-face consistency

- **local-video-director** — Multi-model directing: 14-field shot cards, cutting by the Rule of Six, animatic keyframes and montage rhythm.
- **local-video-face-continuity** — Repairs and locks facial consistency with face swap, head swap and multi-angle locking; regenerates a shot when the face drifts instead of patching it.
- **local-video-shots** — Breaks a finished video down into a per-shot table — duration, shot size, camera movement, framing — so you can study its pacing and find what to fix.

### Working with Flow and Veo

- **local-flow-prompting** — Writes Google Flow / Veo 3.1 prompts in a five-part formula, with correct audio syntax and timestamped segments.
- **local-ai-cinematographer** — Acts as director of photography: mode selection, coverage planning, keyframe strategy and failure diagnosis, with a clear split between when to use Flow and when to use the API.

### Model selection and prompt repair

- **local-lanshu-model-selector** — Picks the 1–3 best-fit models out of 16 for a scene, based on shot type, duration, audio, language and budget.
- **local-lanshu-prompt-translator** — Translates a prompt from one video model to another using a cross-reference of 10 scene types × 11 models, instead of rewriting by feel.
- **local-lanshu-seedance-storyboard** — Splits a story into multi-scene Seedance storyboards; every scene covers four dimensions: camera, action, position and sound.
- **local-lanshu-seedance-debugger** — Diagnoses 12 common Seedance faults — face drift, stray subtitles, scene jumps, style drift — and returns a corrected prompt.
- **local-lanshu-kling-prompter** — Writes Kling 3.0 prompts in three forms: basic four-part, advanced five-layer, and image-to-video.
- **local-seedance2-creative-cli** — A Seedance creative desk that works from images and text: read the image, expand the text, match the camera, then generate.
- **local-seedance-horror-suspense** — Writes horror and thriller prompts by timecode, with one clear on-body change per shot.
