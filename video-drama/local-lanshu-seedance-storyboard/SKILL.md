# seedance-storyboard

Story → multi-shot storyboard. Inside Seedance, time and space are decoupled, so **the ideal form for a complex video is a timeline-based storyboard**.

*Source: https://github.com/cclank/lanshu-awesome-ai-video-kit (MIT)*

## When not to use this skill

A single shot with a single action goes to the Seedance prompter; a compact 5/10/15-second short clip goes to the HappyHorse prompter; a looping video is handled as a single shot with seamless start and end frames.

## Core rules

Shots are marked `Shot 1`, `Shot 2`, `Shot 3` in the order events happen, without forcing a duration onto each segment. With Seedance's 15-second maximum, 3–5 shots is the most reliable range, and each shot is organized along four dimensions: camera movement or transition, subject action and expression, position or spatial change, and audio information.

## Workflow

Parse the story into 3–5 key beats (one per shot), define the functional role of each reference asset (character anchor, scene setter, camera-movement reference, rhythm and mood — 4–5 assets, not the maximum), assemble the storyboard with the template, run the checklist (one camera movement per shot, stable character labels, explicit subject references, limb-specific actions, concretely externalized emotion), and output the storyboard script with shot count, recommended duration, aspect ratio, a shot-beat analysis, and asset suggestions.
