# kling-prompter

The three mainstream writing approaches for Kling 3.0 (by Kuaishou): the 4-part basic formula / the 5-layer advanced formula / image-to-video.

*Source: https://github.com/cclank/lanshu-awesome-ai-video-kit (MIT)*

## When not to use this skill

Complex multi-shot storyboard narratives (>15s) go to the Seedance storyboard skill; compact short clips where native audio is not a priority go to the HappyHorse prompter; an existing Seedance prompt that needs fixing goes to the Seedance debugger.

## Decision tree for the three approaches

A 5-second single-scene short video uses Approach 1 (4-part basic formula); a story with audio and multiple characters uses Approach 2 (5-layer advanced formula), Kling's strength; animating an existing reference image uses Approach 3 (image-to-video only), also a Kling strength.

## Workflow

The workflow decides the approach from the request's keywords, clarifies 2–3 missing elements when needed, fills in the chosen formula (the 4-part structure, the structured 5-layer Scene / Characters / Action / Camera / Audio & Style format, or motion-only image-to-video), self-checks against the 6 Kling writing rules, chooses Chinese or English without mixing, adds a negative prompt, and outputs the prompt with recommended parameters, the key technique, and adjustable points.

## Worked examples

The full version works through three examples in detail: a transformation viral clip (Approach 1), two old friends reuniting with voiceover (Approach 2), and making a kitchen photo move with a chef tossing a stir-fry (Approach 3).
