# Horror and suspense

Every shot carries its own timecode and shows one visible change on a body. The dread comes from the chain — a look, veins, a bite, the next person — and the ending seals a door without settling anything.

This Skill carries one prompt structure distilled from 11 human-verified Seedance cases on goodcase.ai. It is a sibling of `seedance-prompt-library` (all templates in one Skill); install this one when you only want this kind of clip. Do not invent structure from general video-generation knowledge: follow the structure below and ground the draft in one anchor case from `references/cases.md`.

## Use when

Outbreak and possession clips, corridor chases, ritual scenes — anything where the fear comes from a body changing on a clock, second by second.

## Workflow

Collect the missing inputs, pick one anchor case (never an average of several), then fill the prompt structure block by block: header and reference lock, patient zero, one-change-per-shot escalation, the trigger attack, a compressed second-round transmission, crowd panic and barricade, and an unresolved closing shot. Return the finished prompt as one copy-pasteable block, the anchor case link, and a short verification checklist.

## Guidance (from the cases)

Give every shot a timecode and exactly one change, lock the face in the very first shot, compress the second infection round, land scares on other people's reactions, and refuse to resolve the ending.

## Pitfalls

Do not cram a whole transformation into one shot, buy horror with gore volume, let the middle collapse into an unreadable brawl, or leave the consistency lock until the end — by then the face has already drifted.

## Language

Reply in the user's language; the prompt itself can stay in English when that is what the user's Seedance workflow expects — ask if unclear.

## The structure in brief

A seven-block chain: header with the reference lock, patient zero with one symptom, escalation shots adding one visible change each, the slow-motion trigger, transmission on a shorter clock, panic and barricade, and a sealed-door closing shot with nothing resolved.
